An Integration Test Final Report summarizes the results of the integration testing phase. 
It provides stakeholders, project managers, and developers with clear evidence of how well the combined modules work together and whether the system is stable enough to move to the next phase, such as system or user acceptance testing (UAT).
Structure of a Final Test 
ReportExecutive Summary: High-level overview of the test cycle results and final release recommendation.
Test Overview: Scope of the integration, including tested modules, environments, and versions.
Execution Metrics: Visual and numeric summary of passed, failed, blocked, and skipped test cases.
Defect Summary: Breakdown of discovered bugs, their severity, and their current resolution status.
Conclusion & Recommendations: Final sign-off status and any known outstanding risks.
